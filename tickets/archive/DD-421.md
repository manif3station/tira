# DD-421

## Title

Media previews: video/audio players, byte-range streaming, TIFF
fallback.

## Resolution

Video and audio attachments open in native players; the attachment route
supports HTTP byte ranges (206) so iOS plays media and seeking works;
TIFF previews where the browser decodes it and otherwise shows a clear
not-supported panel with the download; PDFs keep the frame; office
documents remain downloads by design (no browser renders them natively).
Guards cover content types, Range semantics, the player pane, and the
fallback panel. Shipped in 0.32.

# DD-417

## Title

iPhone drag: block native touch scrolling while a drag is armed.

## Problem-Solving Loop record

1. **Expected:** hold-to-drag tracks the finger on a real iPhone.
2. **Problem:** owner screen recording: the drag arms (ghost + target
   highlight) but the ghost never follows the finger.
3. **Root cause:** all iOS browsers are WebKit, which keeps claiming the
   moving touch for scrolling; pointer-event preventDefault does not stop
   that — only preventing the raw `touchmove` does. Prior touch proof was
   emulated Chromium (boundary recorded in DD-410).
4-5. **Reproduction:** the owner's recording is the real-device repro;
   emulated Chromium cannot reproduce WebKit arbitration, and the host
   cannot launch the Playwright WebKit port (missing system libraries).
6. **Fix:** non-passive document `touchmove` listener prevents default
   only while `dragState.started`; pointercancel still cleans up;
   `-webkit-touch-callout:none` on cards.
7. **Re-run:** owner re-tested on the physical iPhone and confirmed
   ("Fixed. Thanks", message 2905, 2026-08-06); Chromium flows green.
8. **Guard:** renderer contract test requires the non-passive blocker.

## Status

Done — confirmed on the owner's physical iPhone (message 2905).

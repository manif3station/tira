# DD-489

## Title

Questions were buried at the bottom of the card.

## Source

Owner: the questions section sat after the comments, so on a card with
ten or twenty comments he had to scroll to the very bottom to find the
one thing needing an answer — on every card, of eighty.

## What I did, and where I went further than asked

He asked for it above the comments. I checked the real order in a
browser and found a dozen sections between the top of the card and the
comments, so "above the comments" alone would still have left it a long
scroll away and not solved what he actually complained about.

It now sits **second, directly after Details**: what the card is, then
what is blocking it, then everything else. That satisfies his
constraint and his reason for it. Stated here so he can move it back if
he disagrees.

Sections are now named in the markup, so one can be found without
matching on its heading text.

# DD-485

## Title

A board toggle for answers waiting to be judged.

## Source

Owner: "at the board control. add a toggle, show only the cards have
questions now been answered and not be marked a done and toggle off to
show everything".

## What it is, and what it is not

It is the agent's review queue: the owner has answered, and the agent
has not yet said whether the answer settles it.

It is deliberately **narrower than the yellow card**. Yellow means
somebody is waiting in either direction, including questions the owner
has not answered — and those are his to deal with, not the agent's.
Putting them in the same list would mean an agent scrolling past work
it cannot act on, which is how a filter stops being used.

A mark of either kind clears a card from the list: a cross is a
judgement too. A discarded question needs none.

## Design

- Off by default, because a board must show all the work until somebody
  narrows it.
- The toggle narrows `renderColumns`, which already owns filtering,
  paging and the counts in one pass — so the counts cannot disagree
  with what is on screen. That was checked in the browser, not assumed.
- Every board carries the control and they act together, like the
  keyword filter.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Only answered-and-unmarked cards survive the toggle.
2. A question the owner has not answered does not qualify.
3. Either mark clears the card; a discarded question never qualifies.
4. Off by default, and switching off restores every card.
5. The counts agree with what is shown.

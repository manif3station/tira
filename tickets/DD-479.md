# DD-479

## Title

A question worth answering says why it is being asked.

## Epic

EPIC-469 (owner, messages 3175 and 3176).

## The gap

A question is currently just its text. But the agent asking usually
knows two things the owner needs and cannot see:

- **why** it is asking — what it is blocked on, what it already tried;
- **what options it can see**, so the owner can pick one rather than
  compose an answer from nothing.

Without those the owner is answering a question stripped of its
context, which is slower for him and produces worse answers.

Second gap: the card dialog has no questions section at all, so the
person who owns the decision cannot see or answer a question in the
place he already reads the card.

## Design

- `reason` is free text, optional. `options` is a list, optional and
  repeatable.
- Both render in the human view under the question, and in the card
  dialog, so an owner reading either sees the same thing.
- Options are presented as a numbered list, because an owner answering
  "2" is the fastest useful answer and the agent can read it.
- Nothing becomes required: a bare question is still a valid question.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A question can carry a reason and any number of options.
2. Both are optional, and a question without them behaves as before.
3. Both appear in the human view, under the question they belong to.
4. Both are available to the browser, and the card dialog shows a
   questions section with them.
5. The owner can answer from the card dialog.
6. `SKILLS.md` and `docs/commands.md` document both.

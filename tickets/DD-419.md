# DD-419

## Title

Center the modal in the viewport and clamp its body to vertical scroll.

## Problem-Solving Loop record

1. **Expected:** the card dialog opens centered on screen wherever the
   page is scrolled, and its body scrolls vertically only.
2. **Problem:** owner recording (message 2910): after scrolling down,
   opening a card shows a blank dark screen (looks broken); inside the
   modal the middle pans left and right.
3. **Root cause:** DD-407 set `position:relative` on the dialog for the
   viewer overlay, cancelling the UA's fixed modal centering — the dialog
   rendered at the page top, off-viewport when scrolled. Separately, long
   unwrapped content exceeded the phone width, enabling sideways panning.
4-5. **Reproduction:** frames from the recording show both; the mobile
   guard reproduces the scrolled-open case.
6. **Fix:** explicit `position:fixed; inset:0; margin:auto` on the
   dialog; `overflow-x:hidden` + `overflow-wrap:anywhere` in the body.
7. **Re-run:** guards pass ×3; owner device check requested.
8. **Guard:** scrolled-open viewport assertion + zero-horizontal-scroll
   assertion in the permanent mobile pass.

## Status

Shipped in 0.30 — awaiting the owner's device confirmation.

# DD-478

## Title

The human view of questions showed a blank card and four warnings.

## Source

Reported by the **developer-dashboard** project, which has adopted the
question commands as its only channel for asking its owner things:
`tickets/REPORT-2026-08-09-question-list-human-output.md`. Filed as a
report rather than a numbered ticket because that project could not
read this board to allocate a number — see the permissions finding
below, which turned out to be real.

## The two defects, and why they are one mistake

Both are in the human renderer, and both come from the same oversight:
I added a new payload shape and never looked at what `-o human` did
with it.

1. **`question_list` was rendered as a card.** Its payload carries a
   `ref`, and the renderer treats anything with a `ref` as a record. So
   it printed `# DD-522:` with an empty title, warned three times on
   the record fields that a question list does not have, and never
   printed the questions at all. The JSON was correct throughout, which
   is exactly what localised it to the renderer.

2. **Every human render warned** on `$data->{description} ne ''` when a
   card had no description. Cosmetic on its own — but it is on stderr
   of every human read, and the real cost of that is teaching people to
   ignore this tool's warnings.

## Why it mattered

UC-101 and UC-102 exist so a card carrying an unanswered question is
visibly waiting on the person who owns the decision. That person reads
human output, not JSON. They saw an empty card and four warnings, so
the one workflow these commands were added for did not reach its
intended reader. The reporter's framing is right and I have kept it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `question.list -o human` lists each question, its answer underneath,
   and the instruction line.
2. A card with no questions says so, rather than printing an empty
   card.
3. No uninitialized-value warning on any human render, including a card
   with no description.
4. Records still render exactly as they did.

# DD-487

## Title

A toggle for the owner's own queue.

## Source

Owner photo, message 3197, circling the board control: "I need another
button, question to be answered".

## The symmetry that was missing

`DD-485` gave the agent a way to find its work — answers it has been
given but not judged. The owner had no equivalent: to find the
questions waiting on him he had to scan the board for yellow.

Now both sides have one, named for whose queue it is:

- **Questions to answer** — the yellow cards, his.
- **Answers to review** — the greyed cards, the agent's.

## Design decision, stated so it can be overruled

The two toggles are **independent, not mutually exclusive**. Turning
one on does not switch the other off, and with both on the board shows
every card with something still open.

I chose that because a button that silently un-presses another is
surprising, and because "everything unsettled" is a genuinely useful
third view rather than a meaningless state. It also makes the mechanism
one thing rather than two: each button contributes its own set, and the
filter is their union.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Questions-to-answer shows exactly the cards waiting on the owner.
2. Answers-to-review shows exactly the cards waiting on the agent.
3. Neither switches the other off.
4. Both on shows the union; both off shows the whole board.
5. The counts agree with what is displayed.

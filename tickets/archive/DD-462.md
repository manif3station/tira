# DD-462

## Title

The escalating reminder itself.

## Epic

EPIC-457 — stale-card reminders.

## Owner decision this implements

Settled point 8: the tone escalates with how many times a card has been
reported in its current column — plain, tense, angry, shouting, and
onward from templates — and resets when the card moves. Settled point
1: one message covering every stale card, not one message per card.

**The wording is the owner's to approve.** This ticket builds the
mechanism and proposes words; nothing sends until he has seen them.

## Decision I had to make, stated for him to overrule

One message covers several cards, and those cards are at different
levels — one on its first reminder, another on its fourth. So which
tone does the message take?

The most-nagged card sets the tone, and every card line states its own
count. A message is as angry as its worst card, so a chronically stuck
card is never softened by newer company, and the agent can still see at
a glance which cards are new and which have been ignored for weeks.

## Design

- `notification_message` takes the stale scan and returns the level,
  the tone name, the text ready to send, and the cards it covers, so
  the collector composes and records from one call.
- Nothing stale means an empty message at level 0 — the collector sends
  nothing rather than sending "all clear" noise on every heartbeat.
- Level 5 and beyond reuse the last template with the real count, so
  escalation never runs out of words or invents a sixth tone.
- Durations are said the way a person would: minutes, then hours, then
  days.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each level produces its own tone: plain, tense, angry, shouting,
   final.
2. Level 5 and beyond reuse the final template and state the real
   count.
3. The message level is the highest level among the cards it covers.
4. Every card appears with its reference, title, column, how long it
   has sat there, and its own count.
5. Nothing stale returns level 0, empty text, and no cards.
6. Durations read as minutes, hours or days as appropriate.
7. `tira.notify.compose` returns the whole thing for the collector.

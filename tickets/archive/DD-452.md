# DD-452

## Title

Cards still spilled between columns on boards with many columns.

## Method

Browser BDD (Playwright) under the Mandatory Problem-Solving Loop, at
the owner's real column count.

## Problem-Solving Loop record

1. **Expected:** a card never draws outside its own column, at any
   column count or width mode.
2. **Problem:** owner photo (message 3007) still showed cards
   overlapping their neighbours after `0.60`. First hypothesis — that he
   had not restarted — was checked and rejected: the installed copy was
   `0.60` (written 01:32) and his dashboard process started at 05:26,
   so he was running the fix.
3. **Root cause:** a different defect from DD-451, in a different mode.
   The card list is a CSS grid and a grid item's default `min-width` is
   `auto`, meaning it refuses to shrink below its own content. DD-451
   fixed the *columns*; this is the *cards*. Once a column is narrower
   than a card's longest word — which happens in Fit mode past about ten
   columns — the card stops fitting and draws over the next column.
   `overflow-wrap: break-word` does not help, because it does not change
   an element's minimum content size; only removing the floor does.
4. **Reproduction:** thirteen columns at 1280px in Fit mode — his board
   has more columns than the nine-column fixture DD-451 added.
   Measured: 81px cells holding 96px cards, spilling 21px.
5. **Pre-fix comparison:** reverting reproduces it
   (`cards spilled in fit mode by [14…]px`).
6. **Fix:** `min-width: 0` on the card list, its items, and the card, so
   they shrink with their column; plus `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the
   reference in Fit mode, since a reference like `M5T-001` is a single
   unbreakable token wider than a very narrow column.
7. **Re-run:** no spill at thirteen columns in either mode; Standard
   still 272px, Fit 81px.
8. **Guard:** the DD-451 wide-board guard now also runs the
   thirteen-column fixture, and fails on the pre-fix stylesheet.

## Honest limit

The overflow is fixed, but thirteen columns across a 1280px window is
81px each — legible only just, with words breaking mid-way. That is
arithmetic, not a defect: "fit all" means every column on screen, and
thirteen columns cannot be both fully visible and comfortable at that
width. The owner's own suggestion — letting the columns out of the board
section so they can wrap onto more than one row — is the right answer to
that, and is raised separately rather than folded in here, because it
replaces the table layout that drag-and-drop, the sticky headers, and
the column dropdown all read.

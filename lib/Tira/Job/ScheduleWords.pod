=head1 NAME

Tira::Job::ScheduleWords - the wording-formatting half of cron schedule
phrasing, lifted out of Tira::Job::Schedule

=head1 DESCRIPTION

C<_ordinal>, C<_weekday_phrase>, C<_monthday_phrase>, C<_about>,
C<_even_step>, C<_and_list>, and the C<@DAY>/C<@MONTH>/C<%DAY_NAME>/
C<%MONTH_NAME> word tables - the self-contained formatting cluster
C<job_schedule_words> (still in C<Tira::Job::Schedule>, since it needs that
module's own cron-parsing internals) calls by fully-qualified name. TKT-1151,
the same mechanical-lift shape TKT-1044 itself used.

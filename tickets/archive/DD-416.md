## Title

Full timestamps on attachment rows.

## Problem

Owner annotation (message 2894): rows showed only the date, so same-day
attachments had no visible order. Sorting already compared full ISO
stamps; only the display truncated.

## Resolution

formatter; the permanent browser guard requires the full stamp. Shipped
in 0.27.

# DD-434

## Title

Indexed reads on the gate log and evidence (CA20).

## Method

Engine TDD + CLI ATDD, red-first (`t/35-log-windows.t`).

## Problem

EPIC-424: the two append-only logs grow without limit and are among the
largest fields on a mature ticket, while the commonest question — "what
did it last pass?" — needs one entry.

## Design

- One `_indexed_log_read` serves both logs from a `%LOG_SPEC` (label,
  unbounded-text field, known entry fields): `--last`/`--first` windows
  over the newest-last storage order with the comment-window semantics
  (mutually exclusive, negative refused, zero = `{count}`, oversize
  safe), `--id` returning the single entry with a loud miss,
  `--meta-only` replacing the unbounded text with `<text>_length` and
  `annotations` with `annotation_count` while keeping results, uris,
  authors, and stamps, and `--where` entry filtering through the
  record-where machinery generalized with a per-log field table.
- Annotations always ride with their parent entry (the "separately on
  request" half of UC9 is served by `--id`); reads never mutate.
- CLI guards extended with substring-stable messages; checklist reads
  are documented as out of scope (bounded lists, no unbounded text).

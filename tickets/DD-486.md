# DD-486

## Title

The board says whose move it is, not just that somebody is waiting.

## Source

Owner: "when all questions being answered on a card and pending for the
agent to mark as done. The card color changes to bright orange instant
yellow".

## The refinement

Yellow meant "somebody is waiting", in either direction. That is one
colour doing two jobs, and it makes the board answer the wrong
question: a person scanning it learns that a card is stuck but not
who is holding it up.

Now:

- **Yellow** — a question nobody has answered. The **owner** owes the
  next move.
- **Orange** — every question answered, at least one not yet ticked or
  crossed. The **agent** owes the next move.
- **No colour** — every question settled, or none asked.

## What made this worth doing carefully

The two states are now **mutually exclusive by construction**: a card
with any unanswered question cannot be orange, however many of its
other answers are unjudged. Without that a card could carry both
colours and the board would be claiming two people owe the same thing.
That is asserted directly.

It also removed a duplication I had just created: `_card_waiting` and
`_card_blocked` ended up with identical bodies, one for the colour and
one for the collector. Two copies of a rule drift, so there is one rule
and both callers ask it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An unanswered question makes a card yellow and never orange.
2. All answered with something unmarked makes it orange and never
   yellow.
3. Marked either way, or discarded, leaves no colour.
4. The review toggle shows exactly the orange cards.
5. Both manuals say what each colour means.

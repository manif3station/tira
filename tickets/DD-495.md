# DD-495

## Title

A judged question collapses to a verdict.

## Source

Owner: "For the marked ok or not ok question. Collapse it to save
space. Just show the question & answer and the final mark in icon
✅❌". And immediately after: sort them, unanswered first, answered
without a verdict next, judged last.

## What changed

A question that has been marked is finished business. It keeps its
question, its answer and a tick or a cross, and loses the apparatus for
acting on it — the reason, the choices, the play control, the answer
box, the mark buttons. All of that is only in the way once there is
nothing left to do. Measured on a real card: 113px against 398px.

The panel is then ordered by what still needs doing — unanswered,
answered but unjudged, judged, set aside — and sorted on a copy, so
displaying a card never reorders the card itself.

## What eyeballing caught that the tests did not

The answer box was meant to stay hidden until somebody presses
**Other…**, and it was on screen the whole time. A `display: flex` rule
beats the `hidden` attribute, which is a trap this dashboard has fallen
into before. Every assertion passed; the panel was simply wrong.

That is the argument for looking at a layout change rather than reading
its test: a test can only check what somebody thought to assert, and
nobody asserts the thing they have not noticed.

The icons are written as `✅` and `❌` rather than literally,
because literal glyphs in this renderer arrive double-encoded — the
defect in `DD-468`.
